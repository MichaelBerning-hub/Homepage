Lernwirksam – AI & Instructional Design

Solve concrete learning problems. Reduce the burden on teaching and training teams. Strengthen independent competence.

Lernwirksam – AI & Didactics · Project idea by Michael Berning · 6 November 2026

The starting point

I am developing a practical concept for schools, universities, adult education providers and companies. It starts with concrete learning problems: what makes understanding, practice or application difficult? Instructional methods and appropriate AI support are selected on that basis. Fast AI-generated results do not prove that learners have understood or can apply the material independently. We therefore start with a specific learning challenge, not a tool.

AI-supported learning and instructional value

AI may support adapted practice, accessible explanations and feedback. Learning support should offer questions and hints that encourage independent thinking. Expert review, transparent use and joint evaluation are part of the concept. Any benefit must be tested in each pilot.

AI literacy: a competence overview

AI literacy brings together knowledge, practical skills and reflective attitudes. For Lernwirksam, the AILit framework provides a reference for planning learning activities. The four domains are intended to inform the selection of learning goals, practice activities and reflection questions.

Understand. Create. Decide. Shape.

Four connected domains, with people retaining responsibility.

Planned fields

Schools

Learning tasks grounded in instructional design to support subject understanding and independent thinking.

Higher education

Learning activities for university study and teaching, intended to support reasoning and independent work.

Adult education

A focus is vocational German courses (DaF/DaZ). A companion app is planned for participants who want to develop their German for work.

Corporate learning

Practical learning modules for employees and support for in-house training teams. A further focus is an AI coaching simulation for professional development, which I am preparing as a work sample.

Blue-collar learning

Accessible, potentially multilingual learning aids for onboarding and technical vocational training.

Planned project · Adult education

Planned adult education project

App for vocational German courses (DaF/DaZ)

A companion app is planned for participants in vocational German courses (DaF/DaZ) who want to develop their German for workplace situations. It is intended to complement classroom learning and support independent practice.

Potential content includes job-related vocabulary, accessible tasks and practice for typical workplace communication. Specific features and trials with participants will be developed as a next step.

Planned project · AI & instructional design

AI training for educators

Practical professional development is planned for educators in schools, higher education and adult education. The focus is on using AI meaningfully to prepare, design and support learning.

Potential topics include improving existing learning materials, critically assessing AI outputs and designing tasks that encourage independent thinking. Needs, formats and potential pilot dates will be explored with interested educational institutions.

Work sample in preparation · Corporate learning

AI coaching: a simulation for corporate learning

I am preparing an AI coaching simulation as a practical work sample. It is intended to demonstrate how people could be supported in learning and reflecting on the use of AI at work.

The project belongs to corporate learning, focusing on employee development within organisations. Planned elements include a specific workplace scenario, learning goals, guided practice with AI tools and a joint review of the results. The simulation is still being developed; it does not represent coaching delivered to clients or demonstrated effectiveness.

Planned project · AI & instructional design

Socratic AI learning companion

A learning companion that teaches people to use AI through questions rather than ready-made answers. Learners write their own prompts, check the results and derive their own rules.

It builds on the Socratic dialogue: clarify the goal, test one's own assumptions and formulate a better solution. Learning always starts from a real task of the learner. Versions are planned for schools, adult education and German as a foreign language, corporate learning and education management. The concept is in progress; no tested prototype exists yet.

Start small, evaluate together

Improve one existing learning module, test it with a small group and evaluate application, independence, access and staff time. Building a new platform is not a prerequisite.

International collaboration and multilingual learning

I welcome exchanges with education and training teams working in German, English and Spanish. Potential projects include jointly developed learning modules and multilingual support for learners with different starting points. Translation alone is insufficient: terminology, cultural context and clarity should be reviewed with the relevant learners.

Separate development project: Karrierekompass

Alongside Lernwirksam, I am developing an AI-supported career and continuing education assistant. It aims to help people with different backgrounds identify competencies, explore career options and plan their next learning steps. This development project is separate from the Lernwirksam pilots.

Research and doctoral perspective

A possible continuation of my earlier DBU-supported doctoral project connects education for sustainable development (ESD), biodiversity and the South American concept of Buen Vivir. The earlier project aimed to gather evidence relevant to the sustainable use of biodiversity and originally also addressed school education. A future research question is how responsible digital learning might support these educational processes. No new doctorate, supervision or funding has been agreed.
